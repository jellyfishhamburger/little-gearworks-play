HKU · IDAT7220 STEAM Education

About the Course

STEM Topic

Renewable energy, mechanical power transmission, and automated transport.

STEM Branches

Science explores energy conversion, motion, and load. Technology and Engineering involve building and debugging a power and transport system. Mathematics is used to reason about tooth-count ratios and rotational speed.

Game Description

Little Gearworks is a 3D engineering game set at a riverside mill. Help Mara, the village baker, by connecting a waterwheel to a mill, matching the mill’s target speed with gears, and building a conveyor line around a warehouse. The gearboxes, rollers, and belts respond to the mechanical connections the player builds.

Learning Outcomes

  1. Explain how flowing water drives a wheel and apply coaxial transmission to power the mill.
  2. Use a gear pair to achieve a target speed and direction, then diagnose and revise a stalled or misaligned drivetrain.
  3. Design and test a powered conveyor line that routes five flour sacks from the mill to the bakery.

Learning Through Play

Build, observe, and revise. The game provides evidence through visible motion, order completion, and gearbox repair. Players can review that evidence and explain design changes in the optional Learning Record.
